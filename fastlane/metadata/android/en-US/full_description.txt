StudyDesk is a study execution app for students who lose time deciding what to study instead of studying.

You enter your courses and deadlines. StudyDesk's Next Up engine ranks what to do right now — so the decision is already made when you sit down.

* Next Up — a rule-based priority list from your deadlines
* Periods / jaksot — organise courses by school year and period
* Period History — archived courses grouped by year and period, with a GPA snapshot
* Grades — IB and GPA scales
* Lock In — a built-in Pomodoro focus timer
* Optional AI study debrief after a session

Warm paper aesthetic, not another dashboard. Your data lives on your device; cloud sync is optional and account-gated. No ads, no trackers, no Google Play Services — open and F-Droid friendly.

Part of the Limecore Nexus suite.
