Fixes: grades of 100 or more now sync to your other devices. They used to stay on the device you entered them on; any that got stuck upload by themselves the next time you open the app. The web and desktop apps, and the phone app when you switch back to it, now fetch changes from your other devices when you return to them, instead of only after a restart. The course you add while setting up the app now syncs too, along with everything you added to it.
