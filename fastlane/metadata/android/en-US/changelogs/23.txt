Fixes syncing, and makes the timer match how people actually study.

Some courses never reached the server, blocking everything queued behind them. The app now spots what is missing and sends it, so a stuck sync clears itself.

The timer takes any length up to four hours, saves your time if you stop early instead of discarding it, and has a stopwatch. A running block shows at the top of every screen.

You can also set a display name and pick a symbol, colour or photo.
